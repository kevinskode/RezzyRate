About

Resume feedback that tells you what to fix next

Rezzy Rate is a fast, friendly resume checker that helps you speak the language of recruiters and applicant tracking systems (ATS), without sounding like a robot.

  • Private by design
  • Results in seconds
  • Plain-language fixes

Why Rezzy Rate exists

Job hunting is stressful enough. Between tailoring your resume, decoding job descriptions and wondering whether a person will ever read your application, it’s easy to feel stuck.

Rezzy Rate makes that process more predictable. Instead of guessing what matters, you get clear, prioritized feedback on the things recruiters and ATS pay attention to: here’s what’s strong, here’s what’s weak, and here’s exactly what to fix next.

What we check

  • Keyword match: how well your resume lines up with the job description
  • Writing quality: clarity, tone, readability and passive voice
  • Structure: the core sections recruiters expect
  • Skimmability: bullets, metrics and line length
  • Impact: whether your bullets show results, not just duties

Who it’s for

  • Career starters: students, recent grads and career changers
  • Experienced pros turning solid experience into sharp bullet points
  • Active job seekers who want a quick check before every application

How it works

  1. Upload or paste your resume. PDF, DOCX or plain text. It’s read in your browser.
  2. Add the job description (recommended). We compare your resume against the keywords the employer is scanning for.
  3. Get your breakdown. A score, the gaps that matter most, specific fixes, and live jobs that fit your background.

What makes it different

  • Plain-language feedback, not dense reports
  • Private: your resume isn’t uploaded to our servers
  • No account and no subscription. Try it free every day

Our promise

The goal isn’t to “game” the system. It’s to help you present your real experience in a way that’s sharp, honest and easy to say “yes” to.

FAQ

Do I have to be a “tech person” to use it?

Not at all. If you can upload a file or paste text, you can use Rezzy Rate. The feedback is written in everyday language.

Does Rezzy Rate write my resume for me?

No. It highlights what’s strong, what’s unclear and where to add detail, so your resume still sounds like you.

Can I use it for multiple applications?

Yes. It works best when you tailor your resume for each role and re-check it against each job description.

Is it a guarantee I’ll get interviews?

No tool can guarantee that. Rezzy Rate improves your odds by helping you present a clearer, more targeted version of your experience.
