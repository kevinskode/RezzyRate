FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my resume uploaded or stored?

No. Your resume is read and scored right in your browser. If you use job matching, only the role, a short list of skills and the location are sent so we can look up listings.

What’s the difference between a free and a paid scan?

A free scan shows your overall score and the four sub-scores, plus a preview of your top fix. A paid scan unlocks everything: the full keyword breakdown, structure checklist, all fixes, the job ad reality check, a written summary and live jobs matched to your resume.

How do credits work?

Each analysis uses one credit. Credits are saved in the browser you bought them in, so use the same browser and device to spend them. Your free scan resets every day.

Is this a subscription?

No. Every purchase is a one-time payment, and you’ll never be charged again automatically.

Will a high score guarantee interviews?

No tool can promise that. Rezzy Rate helps you present your experience clearly and match the language employers are scanning for, which improves your odds.

I need help with a purchase.
